# Dogwatch

**Dogwatch is an always-on, private assistant that keeps a seafarer's certificates under his control and has every renewal ready before a lapse costs him a contract.**

Arjun is on the four-to-eight watch in the Arabian Sea. His ship has twenty minutes of internet a day. A manning agent has just offered him six months of work, and one of his certificates runs out in the middle of it. Dogwatch finds that out tonight, on his own laptop, without his documents leaving it.
