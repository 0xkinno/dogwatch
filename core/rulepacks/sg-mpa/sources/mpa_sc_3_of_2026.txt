SEAFARERS, 1978 (STCW 1978), AS AMENDED BY SINGAPORE The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA), as Singapore's maritime administration, and the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA), as Singapore's national telecommunications regulator, issue seafarer’s certificates prescribed under the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers, 1978 (STCW 1978), as amended, under each authority’s respective regulatory frameworks. 2 This circular provides guidance to applicants on the specific requirements for revalidating Singapore-issued STCW certificates required by the respective authorities, as detailed in the following annexes: Annex STCW Certificate Issuing Authority A Deck Officers Certificate of Competency MPA B Marine Engineer E Certificate of Proficiency issued under International Code of Safety for Ships using Gases or other Low-flashpoint Fuels (IGF Code) F Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) CHEAH AUN AUN (MR) DIRECTOR OF MARINE THE MARITIME AND PORT AUTHORITY OF SINGAPORE YUVARANI THANGAVELU (MS) DIRECTOR OF LICENSING & ENFORCEMENT, INVESTIGATION, AND PROSECUTION THE INFOCOMM MEDIA DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY Page 3 of 11 Annex A REQUIREMENTS FOR REVALIDATION OF DECK OFFICER CERTIFICATES OF COMPETENCY (COC) ISSUED BY SINGAPORE Professional competence Continued professional competence for revalidation of Singapore-issued Deck officer COCs shall be established by: a. approved seagoing service, performing functions appropriate to the certificate held, for a period of at least: i) twelve months in total during the preceding five years, or ii) three months in total during the preceding six months immediately prior to revalidating; or b. having performed duties deemed equivalent to seagoing service for a minimum of thirty months within the five years preceding the revalidation application. Occupations considered to provide essential updates in professional knowledge may include marine surveyor, marine officer, marine superintendent, nautical studies lecturer, deck officer examiner, ship surveyor, cargo surveyor, licensed harbour pilot, or other related professions that may be assessed on a case-by-case basis; or c. passing an approved test(s) by MPA; or d. successfully completing an MPA-approved training course(s); or e. having completed approved seagoing service, performing functions appropriate to the certificate held, for a period of not less than three months in a supernumerary capacity, or in a lower officer rank than that for which the certificate held is valid immediately prior to taking up the rank for which it is valid. Annex D Page 8 of 11 THE REQUIREMENTS FOR REVALIDATION OF CERTIFICATE OF PROFICIENCY(COP) ISSUED BY SINGAPORE UNDER THE POLAR CODE Professional competence Continued professional competence for revalidation of Singapore issued COP under the Polar Code shall be established by: a. approved seagoing service, performing functions appropriate to the certificate held, for a period of at least two months in total during the preceding five years; or b. having performed functions deemed equivalent to seagoing service, which will be assessed on a case-by-case basis by MPA; or c. passing an MPA-approved/recognised test; or d. successfully completing an MPA-approved/recognised training course or courses. Note: • MPA may accept certificates issued by or on behalf of administrations that have established STCW certificate recognition arrangements with Singapore (refer to relevant shipping circular for specific details). • If accepted, MPA would not issue a separate endorsement for the recognition of COPs that are issued by foreign maritime administrations. Page 9 of 11 Annex E REQUIREMENTS FOR REVALIDATION OF CERTIFICATE OF PROFICIENCY (COP) ISSUED BY SINGAPORE UNDER THE IGF CODE Professional competence Continued professional for revalidation of Singapore issued COP under the IGF Code shall be established by: a. approved seagoing service, performing duties appropriate to the IGF COP held, for a period of at least 3 months in total during the preceding 5 years; or b. successfully completing an MPA-approved/recognised relevant training course or courses. Notes: • MPA may accept certificates issued by or on behalf of administrations that have established STCW certificate recognition arrangements with Singapore (refer to relevant shipping circular for specific details). • If accepted, MPA would not issue a separate endorsement for the recognition of COPs that are issued by foreign maritime administrations.
