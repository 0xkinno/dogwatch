Summary 
This MIN has been re-issued (previously MIN 443). Where officers cannot comply with the 
revalidation requirements laid down in MGN 9 (M), this MIN offers an alternative. This note 
provides information on how you may sail in a lower than certified capacity in order to 
revalidate your UK Certificates of Competency (CoC). This replaces paragraph 10.6 in MGN 
9 (M).  Applications will be made to Seafarer Training and Certification branch who will issue 
a Notice of Eligibility for an oral examination with a MCA Examiner.   
 
 
1. Introduction 
 
1.1 Marine Guidance Note (MGN) 9 (M) and Marine Information Note (MIN) 4 94 (M) provide 
guidance regarding the revalidation of Certificates of Competency issued in accordance with 
the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping 1978 
as amended. Every Master and Officer holding a Certificate of Competency (CoC) issued 
under STCW who wishes to serve at sea is required to revalidate their CoC at intervals not 
exceeding 5 years. 
 
1.2   As per MGN 9 (M) paragraph 10.0 and MIN 494 paragraph 4.1 in order to revalidate your 
CoC, you must: 
 
(a) Have served as an Officer in your relevant discipline (deck or engineering) for at least 
12 months (which need not be continuous) during the preceding 5 years; or 
(b) Have served as an Officer or supernumerary in your relevant discipline (deck or 
engineering) for at least 3 months in total during the preceding 6 months immediately 
prior to revalidation; or   
(c) Have been engaged in an occupation the MCA considers to be equivalent to the sea 
service required for revalidation for at least 2 and a half years of the preceding 5 years.

1.3  If you do not meet the revalidation criteria you may not be familiar with recent developments. 
The MCA must therefore ensure your competence before revalidating your CoC.    
 
1.4  You may apply to Seafarer Training and Certification branch for a Notice of Eligibility (NOE) 
for an MCA oral examination. On passing the oral examination you will be issued with a 
temporary CoC for service at a lower rank.  After completing 3 months ’ seagoing service 
your CoC  will be revalidated in full.  Temporary Certificates will be issued for a period of 6 
months; they may be extended at the discretion of the Chief Examiner. Those who wish to 
restrict their service to a certain type of vessel ma y elect to be examined only on that type 
of vessel, in which case the temporary CoC will indicate this limitation. 
 
1.5 If you are either a Deck Officer of the Watch or Engineering Officer of the Watch who cannot 
revalidate through the means of MGN 9, MIN 4 94 or paragraph 1.4 above, you may sit a 
FULL Deck Officer of the Watch or Engineering Officer of the Watch oral exam.  On 
successful completion of the oral examination and providing you meet the other revalidation 
requirements, your certificate will be revalidated in full. 
 
2. Alternative Route Application Procedure 
 
2.1 Applications must be submitted to the Seafarer Training and Certification Branch with the full 
supporting documentation specified below. A flow diagram outlining the procedure can be 
found in Annex 1. 
 
2.2 To revalidate your UK CoC you must submit:  
 
(a) A completed and signed MSF 4258 
  
(b) UK CoC and the following ancillary certificates or their recognised equivalents (see 
table below); 
 
 Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Boats other than Fast Rescue Boats 
(PSC & RB) (STCW Code A-VI/2-1); 
 Advanced Fire Fighting (STCW Code A-VI/3); 
 Proficiency in Medical First Aid (STCW Code A-VI/4-1); 
 
(c)  For deck officers only  
 
 Masters and Chief Mates only: certificate of Proficiency in Medical Care (STCW 
A-VI/4-2) or accepted equivalent; 
 For holders of CoCs issued before the STCW 95 amendments came into force: 
Ancillary Certificates as described in MGN 9 (M) paragraph 10.8.1; 
 GMDSS Certificate approved by the MCA 
(d) Attested copy of Passport*; 
(e) 2 passport-size photographs (1 attested as true likeness); 
(f) A valid ENG1 Medical Fitness Certificate, or accepted equivalent; and 
(g) The appropriate fee. 
* You should submit a photocopy of your passport attested by either:   
 
 A Notary Public (UK or Foreign);  
 MCA approved Nautical Colleges; 
 PYA; 
 IYT; 
 Other MCA approved organisations.

The copy should be stamped, signed and sighted as an original copy.  Copies must be clear 
and to scale.  Your original passport and visa (if required) will be checked against your 
Notice of Eligibility at the oral examination. 
2.3 Once you have completed 3 months’ seagoing service in an appropriate capacity you should 
submit your temporary Certificate of Competency (CoC), Discharge Book or Certificates of 
Discharge and Sea Service Testimonials with a covering letter to the Seafarer Training and 
Certification Branch. Once the sea time has been checked the MCA will issue you with a full 
UK CoC.
